# Contributing to Jot

First off, thank you for considering contributing to Jot. It’s people like you that make Jot a better tool for everyone.

Jot is more than just a task manager; it's a **cognitive support environment**. Our goal is to help people feel less scattered and more grounded.

## 🌈 Our Vision

We believe that tools should adapt to the human mind, not the other way around. We value:
- **Softness**: Interactions that feel tactile and gentle.
- **Privacy**: Local-first by default. No tracking, no clouds.
- **Clarity**: Interfaces that whisper instead of shout.

## 🛠 How You Can Help

### 1. Design & UX
- Help us refine the "emotional clarity" of the interface.
- Suggest ways to make transitions smoother and more organic.
- Improve accessibility without sacrificing the minimal aesthetic.

### 2. Engineering
- **Performance**: Ensure the app remains snappy as the local database grows.
- **Features**: Implement features from our roadmap (like "Desk States" or "Return Momentum" refinements).
- **Bugs**: Help us squash any technical debt or UI glitches.

### 3. Community & Content
- Share how Jot has helped you stay calm.
- Help us improve our documentation.

## 🚀 Development Workflow

1.  **Fork** the repository.
2.  **Clone** your fork.
3.  **Install** dependencies with `bun install`.
4.  **Create a branch** for your changes.
5.  **Make your changes** and ensure the code follows our style (Prettier & ESLint).
6.  **Test** your changes.
7.  **Submit a Pull Request**.

## 🎨 Design Guidelines

When adding or changing UI elements:
- Use the **Mesa palette**: Soft stones, muted sages, and warm off-whites.
- Prioritize **spacing** over density.
- If an element feels "loud," try to soften it (reduce contrast, add a shadow, or use a smaller font).
- Ask yourself: *"Does this help a user feel less overwhelmed?"*

## 📜 Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for everyone. Please be kind, respectful, and supportive of your fellow contributors.
